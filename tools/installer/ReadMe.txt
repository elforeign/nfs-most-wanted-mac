NFS Native - Setup

Best of all worlds: your PC game on Mac, with console-inspired features and more from your own Xbox 360 files. Setup contains no game files; nothing is uploaded.
Optional cars in Most Wanted: use your own installed Carbon, ProStreet or
Undercover PC folder, or a Carbon Xbox 360 folder, .iso, or .zip/.7z containing
one of those Xbox 360 sources.
In Setup, open Extras from your other games. For an existing install, choose
Manage installed extras… on the welcome screen. Choose a Later-game source,
tick the cars you want, click Retry check, then Import selected extras.
In the game, press F10 (or Fn+F10), open Cars & cutscenes, turn on Cars from
later games, then quit and reopen the game.
Keep retained generations: if a career needs removed cars, restore its verified
generation or reimport identical inputs with the same package, then restart.
Do not delete the career or its dependency files to bypass a refusal.

You need:
  - An Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later) with macOS @MIN_MACOS@ or later.
  - About 12 GB free while Setup runs, plus time for the first build.

What you can use:
  - Your own installed PC game folder, or pick speed.exe (Most Wanted 1.3) or NFSC.exe (Carbon 1.4).
  - A .zip or .7z of that folder, or original PC discs as .iso images or mounted/inserted discs. Carbon also accepts ISO-compatible .mdf images.
  - For older Most Wanted discs, choose the official 1.3 patch and a compatible 1.3 speed.exe. For Carbon discs, choose the official update for your disc edition and a compatible 1.4 NFSC.exe. Disc executables are copy-protected.
  - Optional: your Xbox 360 files as an .iso, extracted folder, .zip or .7z add console features. You still need the PC game.
  - Have a .rar or .bin/.cue? Unpack it to a folder first.

Opening the app the first time:
  This app is not notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks it the first time. Open it once, click Done,
  then go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway" next to its name.

Then:
  1. Install Apple's Command Line Tools if asked (free).
  2. Choose your PC game using an option above, optionally add your Xbox 360 files, and choose where the app should go.
  3. Click Build. Cancel stops it and leaves anything already installed as it was.
  4. When it finishes, click Open.

Stuck? Setup shows the failed step and Show Log opens the full log. See Troubleshooting or ask on Discord or GitHub.

Your game data and settings: ~/Library/Application Support/NFSMW-port/
Keep Setup's files for updates: ~/Library/Application Support/NFSMW-Setup/
Rebuildable cache: ~/Library/Caches/NFSMW-Setup/
Logs: ~/Library/Logs/NFSMW-Setup/
