NFS Most Wanted Native - Setup

This app builds the native Mac version of Need for Speed: Most Wanted (2005) from YOUR OWN copy of the PC game.
It contains no game files. Everything happens on your Mac; nothing is uploaded.

You need:
  - An Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later).
  - Your own installed PC game folder: Need for Speed: Most Wanted, Black Edition, version 1.3 (English),
    the folder with speed.exe, GLOBAL, TRACKS, MOVIES and so on.
  - About 10 GB free, and about 10 minutes the first time on a recent Mac (longer on older ones).

Opening the app the first time:
  This app is not notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks it the first time. Open it once, click Done,
  then go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway" next to its name.

Then:
  1. Choose your game folder. The app checks your speed.exe first and changes nothing if it can't be used.
  2. If asked, install Apple's Command Line Tools (free; the app waits for them).
  3. Click Build. When it finishes, click "Open the game".

Your saves and settings: ~/Library/Application Support/NFS Most Wanted Native/
The setup's own files (safe to delete after building; an update is quicker with them): ~/Library/NFSMW-Native-Setup/
