# Guide

## Open Help from the couch

Click the touchpad on a DualSense or DualShock 4 to open F10. On a keyboard, press F10, or Fn+F10 if F10 controls your Mac's sound.

Choose **Help > Guide**.

Change the button that opens F10 in **Controls**.

## Move around F10

- D-pad or left stick: choose a row with up/down; change its value with left/right.
- Cross / A selects. L1/R1 or LB/RB changes tabs. Triangle / Y restores a row's default.
- Circle / B closes F10. Options / Start still opens the game's own pause menu.

In this guide, up/down scrolls and the shoulder buttons turn pages. Circle / B returns to Help; F10 closes the menu.

Keyboard: arrows move, Enter selects, Tab changes tabs and Backspace restores a default. In the guide, use PgUp/PgDn, Home/End or the mouse wheel. Esc goes back.

## Choose a look

Open **Picture > Look preset** and choose one of four looks:

- **Port (recommended)**: natural colours, neutral daylight and softer glare, with Xbox 360 effects where available.
- **Xbox 360 retail**: the console's warm colour filter, lighting and effects. This is the choice for the familiar yellow "piss filter".
- **PC retail**: the original PC colour filter, lighting and post-processing. It keeps the PC game's own rendering treatment, including its bright highlights.
- **My picks**: your saved combination of picture settings. Editing a picture row saves it here automatically.

## Finish changing a look

After selecting a retail look, use **Display & performance > Restart game** if rows are awaiting a restart. Save your career first; restarting loses unsaved race progress.

The retail looks set **Colour filter to 100%** and **World lighting to Original**. Port uses a 0% filter and Neutral lighting. The PC and Xbox 360 grades are different.

Both retail looks set **HDR output**, **Contact shadows** and **Soft smoke** to Off. To use those extras, change their picture settings after choosing the look; the edited combination becomes My picks. Restart to apply HDR changes.

Named looks keep your Brightness and Anti-aliasing choices. My picks remembers those too, survives a restart and lets you return after trying another look.

The full Xbox 360 look needs your Xbox 360 files installed through Setup. Missing features stay PC or are hidden; the menu warns when the look is partial.

These are looks, not exact console reproductions. Keep your preferred screen resolution. Audio and driving controls have their own settings.

## Picture

- **Look preset** changes the overall look. **Anti-aliasing** smooths edges: Off, 2x, 4x or 8x, as supported by your Mac.
- **Brightness** adjusts the whole picture, including the HUD, from 0–100%. **HDR output** defaults to Auto: it decides at launch whether the display reports HDR support, using SDR otherwise. Restart to re-check after changing displays or display settings. Off forces SDR; On requests HDR with SDR fallback. Changes need a restart; **HDR status** explains the current output.
- **Headlamp glass** offers Retail (default), keeping the original lamp shading, or Clear for transparent covers over the reflectors. Native PC glass stays unchanged.
- **Post-processing** selects X360 effects or the PC treatment. **Tone curve** changes the brightness curve; **Tone curve glow** changes its spread.
- **Bloom strength** runs from 0–200%; 100% is the normal strength. **Bloom style** changes the glare's shape, and **Highlights** chooses soft Roll off or flat-white Clip. These last two affect X360 post-processing.
- **Colour filter** blends from natural colours at 0% to the game's full grade at 100%. **Banding > Smooth** softens steps in sky gradients. **Speed blur** offers Off, X360 or Strong.

## World

- **World lighting** chooses Neutral daylight or Original lighting. **Lighting data** selects PC or X360 scene lighting; **Scenery lights** adds the matching console lamps.
- **Tunnel shadows** either fade as in the PC game or stay visible with Keep. **Shadow fit** changes how sun shadows cover the scene; shadow quality is in the game's Video options.
- **Grass**, **Tree shading**, **Water**, **Building glass**, **Smoke and dust shading**, and **Wall and kerb depth** choose PC or X360 surface detail and lighting.
- **Road reflection** changes wet-road sharpness. **Rain ripples** adds rings to wet roads; **Raindrops on the lens** chooses console drops or PC drops, including their original placement before post-processing.
- **Detail distance** offers PC, Far (2x) or Max (4x). **World streaming > Ahead** loads scenery earlier. **HD textures** and **UI textures** choose PC or installed X360 textures and need a restart.
- **Smooth cut-out edges** softens fence, leaf and grass cut-outs. It is Off by default and requires **Picture > Anti-aliasing** at **2x or higher**; it has no effect with anti-aliasing Off.

## Cars & cutscenes

- **Car paint** selects the PC highlight or the console's sun-coloured highlight. **Car glass** changes window opacity and reflections.
- **Tyre and badge detail** adds surface relief; **Paint flake** adds close-up sparkle. Their X360 choices need X360 Car paint too.
- **Car reflections > Sharp** adds finer reflected detail and uses more memory; Standard keeps the original. Restart to apply.
- **Police light glow** adds glow around police lights. **Arrest scenes** chooses PC or installed X360 scenes and needs a restart.

## Mirror

- **Mirror brightness** ranges from 50–200%, or Follow main view. 100% is the PC default; the console value is marked X360.
- **Mirror resolution** chooses PC (256x256) or 50%, 67% or 100% of its on-screen size. Higher values make it sharper.
- **Mirror world detail** chooses reduced PC scenery or the same detail as the main view.
- **Mirror view** offers natural views from 45 to 90 degrees, or the PC's wide, squeezed view.
- **Mirror fog** adds distant haze. **Mirror anti-aliasing** smooths edges using the main view's anti-aliasing setting.

## Display & performance

- **Window mode** switches between Full screen and a resizable Window.
- **Full screen** chooses Borderless (default, recommended) or Native. **Known issue with Native:** on some Macs, macOS can drop the graphics chip to its slowest speed during long, busy scenes (big pursuits) and not raise it again, so the frame rate falls to about a third until you leave Native. If that happens, switch to Borderless. For Native, also set **Window mode > Full screen**, save your career and restart. Native uses its own macOS Space so Game Mode can engage; macOS controls whether it does.
- **Frame rate limit** offers Off, 120, 60 or 40. Display refresh affects the result: 40 on a 60 Hz screen gives 30 fps. **Variable refresh pacing** asks compatible displays for adaptive pacing.
- **Performance overlay** shows Compact numbers or Full details and a timing graph while you drive.
- **Quit game** and **Restart game** need a second press within four seconds. Settings are saved; race progress since your last save is lost.
- **Online** holds update actions and **Discord status**. Discord status is off by default and needs Discord running on this Mac.

## Online (beta)

Online free roam is an early beta, Off until you choose Host or Join. Use the same game version on both Macs. Online races are not included. Begin with two players on different internet connections.

Host: enter career free roam, then open **F10 > Online (beta) > Host**. Hosting allows incoming UDP traffic. If macOS asks to allow incoming connections for the game, choose **Allow**. If Local Network access is requested, allow it for router setup.

If you previously denied access, allow the game in **System Settings > Network > Firewall > Options**, and check **Privacy & Security > Local Network**. Keep the firewall enabled.

Send the displayed join code privately to your friend. Keep the game running and the Mac awake. Join codes contain the host’s internet address; they are not passwords.

Join: enter career free roam, open **F10 > Online (beta) > Join code**, enter the nine letters/numbers, then choose **Join**. Close F10 and check that both players see each other’s cars moving. **Leave** disconnects; when the host leaves, the session closes.

A router mapping or join code does not prove a friend can connect. Router loopback failure alone does not mean internet joining will fail. Test from another internet connection.

If joining fails, confirm the host is still hosting, check the latest code and permissions, then try swapping hosts. If automatic router setup fails, forward **UDP 47800** to the host Mac’s local address shown in Status. Joiners normally need no forwarding.

If the public address is unknown, enter your router’s public IPv4 in **Public IPv4 (optional)** after arranging forwarding, then choose Host again. Entering an address does not open a port. With two home routers, both may need configuration; with ISP/shared-address NAT, ask your ISP whether inbound public IPv4 is available.

Some networks cannot host this beta. Try another host or connection. No internet relay fallback is provided in this beta. Remove manual forwarding when finished.

**Ghost mode** lets your car and friends’ cars pass through each other. Toggle it in **F10 > Online (beta) > Ghost mode**, or press **D-pad Down / G** while driving.

Online sessions cap the frame rate at **60 fps** and render at most **six nearby remote cars**. Actual frame rates depend on your Mac, settings and the scene.

Report successes and failures from both Macs: game/macOS versions, Mac and router models, host/joiner roles, separate networks, mapping protocol, last Status and whether cars appeared. Preview reports and inspect every attachment. Remove join codes, addresses and personal details from logs and screenshots before posting.

## Audio

- **Output > Automatic** chooses a supported format. You can also request Dolby TrueHD 5.1, Dolby Digital Plus 5.1, Dolby Digital 5.1, PCM 5.1 or Stereo.
- **Playing now** shows the actual format and device. Check this after changing a TV, receiver or audio connection.
- **Speaker layout** chooses the game's original surround angles or ITU 5.1 angles; restart to apply.
- **Engine sound > Retail** keeps the original placement. **Realistic** places sounds around the car and its engine. **Peak limiter** helps stop loud sounds distorting.
- **Sound mix**, **Reverb**, **Music**, **Police radio** and **Cutscene audio** choose PC or installed X360 sound. Reverb needs a restart. Look presets do not change these.

## Controls

- **Controller: open this menu** chooses the F10 button.
- **Button layout** selects PlayStation or Xbox. **Button prompts** chooses Automatic, Console glyphs or PC keycaps; restart after changing prompts.
- The new **Triggers** selector chooses Shift (the default) or Pedals on a PlayStation controller. Pedals uses R2 for proportional gas, L2 for brake/reverse, Cross to shift up and Square to shift down. Nitrous and handbrake keep their bindings.
- **Controllers > 2 (split-screen)** enables the two-controller option after a restart. Split Screen is in Quick Race; this feature is in development.
- **Rumble** also needs the game's own Rumble option on. Cobbles and prop-hit settings add road texture and impact-based feedback. DualSense adaptive triggers, lightbar, trigger road loading and player indicator LEDs are built in and On by default on supported controllers.
- **Control type** offers X360 driving layouts; Type 1 keeps your bindings and Types 6–10 steer with the D-pad. **Steering dead zone**, **Steering response** and **Speed-reactive chase camera** change stick feel and camera movement.

## Driving layouts

**PlayStation** follows the PS2 layout: Cross accelerates, Square brakes, L1 uses nitrous and R1 is the handbrake. The right stick up/down also gives analog gas/brake. R2/L2 shift gears.

**Xbox** uses RT for gas, LT for brake, A for handbrake, B for nitrous, X for speedbreaker and Y to look behind.

Before you choose a layout, the active controller uses its own console's layout. If gas is on A and the triggers shift gears, choose **Controls > Button layout > Xbox**.

On a PlayStation controller, choose **F10 > Controls > Triggers > Pedals** for trigger pedals, or keep Shift and customize bindings in the game's **Options > Controls**. Returning to Shift restores your saved bindings. On DualSense, a trigger bound to gas has a light rising spring, brake gets firmer late in its travel, and gear shifts keep their click. These effects follow the binding automatically.

## Type with a controller

The on-screen keyboard appears when you select a text field with a controller, including a profile name or the Help report's Note.

- Move with the D-pad or left stick. **Cross / A** types the selected character.
- **Circle / B** deletes. **Square / X** accepts the text. **Triangle / Y** cancels.
- **L1 / LB** changes letter case; **R1 / RB** changes to symbols.

You can also use your Mac keyboard. Follow the displayed buttons: text entry uses different actions from F10 navigation.

## Gameplay

- **Markers per Rival** chooses 1–6 reward cards after a Blacklist win; the original game allows 2. **Heat Level > Game** follows the career, or choose a fixed level from 1–6.
- **No Catch-up > On** stops rivals speeding up just because they are behind. Off keeps the original rubber banding.
- **Black Edition Content** enables its bonus content. **Starting Bonus ($10,000)** pays once when you choose a career's first car. The skip options skip the career prologue or startup movies.
- **LAN play (beta)** needs a restart and Macs on the same network. **Language**, when shown, selects installed text languages; Auto follows your Mac where possible. Speech stays as installed.
- **Unlock All** and **Open Map** affect your save or career progression. Back up your career before experimenting; turning an option off does not undo everything you bought or did.

## Help

- **Guide** opens this document. **Last second** and **Audio output** show recent performance and sound information.
- Under **Report a problem**, choose **What's wrong** and add an optional **Note** describing what happened.
- **Save report** collects a screenshot, recent performance, logs and settings. **Show reports in Finder** opens the saved folder.
- In public builds, **Send to GitHub** and **Post on Discord** preview report text before opening an issue draft or copying text to paste. You submit it and add attachments yourself.
- **Show logs in Finder** opens the current logs. **Full diagnostic logging** collects extra detail from the next launch; turn it off after reproducing the problem.

## Update the game

Open **Display & performance > Online > Check for updates**. It checks GitHub when you press it.

When an update is available, select **Update now**. It downloads and prepares the update for the next launch. Follow the status, then quit and reopen the game when it is ready.

**Cancel update** stops a download, preparation or ready update before the next launch. **Retry update** starts again after a failure or cancellation. **Open log** shows details if something goes wrong.

Update now needs the original PC and any selected Xbox 360 sources remembered by Setup to remain available. If they have moved, run the latest Setup again and select them. Players on 4.2.0 use its browser link to download the new Setup, quit the game and run Setup manually; 4.2.0 does not download or prepare updates itself. Careers and settings are kept.

## Report a problem

Open **Help > Report a problem**, choose **What's wrong**, add a **Note** and select **Save report**. Saving a report does not send it anywhere.

In public builds, choose **Send to GitHub** or **Post on Discord** to preview the report text. GitHub opens an issue draft; Discord copies text for you to paste in **#bug-reports**. You submit the post and add attachments yourself.

Use **Show reports in Finder** to find attachments. Review the text, screenshots and logs for personal details before posting. Include your Mac model, macOS and game versions, what you did, what you expected and what happened. Please do not attach game files.

## Find logs without opening the game

In Finder, choose **Go > Go to Folder** and enter:

~/Library/Logs/NFSMW-port/

**NFSMW.log** is the newest run; **NFSMW.previous.log** is the run before it. Include the newest log if the game quits at launch.

Setup logs are in **~/Library/Logs/NFSMW-Setup/**. Use Setup's **Show Log** when installation fails.

## FAQ: Why don't the triggers accelerate?

The PS2 layout uses face buttons for gas/brake and triggers for gears. Choose **Controls > Button layout > Xbox** on an Xbox controller. On PlayStation, choose **Controls > Triggers > Pedals** for R2 gas and L2 brake/reverse.

## FAQ: Are remapped trigger pedals analog?

Yes; remapped proportional triggers were already supported in 4.2. R2/L2 pressure is proportional from release to full travel, with the game's own small release/full-press clamp. On PlayStation, **Controls > Triggers > Pedals** sets R2 gas and L2 brake/reverse; binding Accelerate and Brake to R2/L2 in the game's **Options > Controls** works too. If they still feel like on/off buttons, report your controller model, USB/Bluetooth connection, layout and bindings.

## FAQ: Why won't F10 open?

Try **Fn+F10** on a Mac keyboard, or click a PlayStation controller's touchpad. You can choose another controller shortcut in Controls once the menu is open.

## FAQ: Why is PC retail so bright or yellow?

It uses the PC game's original colour treatment at full strength. Choose **Port (recommended)** for a clearer look, or lower **Colour filter** to taste. That edit becomes My picks.

## FAQ: Why are some Xbox 360 settings missing?

They need files from your own Xbox 360 copy. A PC-only installation can still play, but cannot show every console effect. Setup accepts a complete Xbox 360 folder or an Xbox 360 .iso.

## FAQ: Which PC folder does Setup need?

Choose the installed folder containing **speed.exe**, GLOBAL, TRACKS, CARS, FRONTEND and SOUND, or a .zip of that folder. Choose the folder from one level up if the Choose button is grey.

A PC installer or disc image is not an installed game folder. The executable must contain the PC game's version 1.3 code.

## FAQ: Why does macOS block Setup?

Setup is not notarized by Apple. Dismiss the message, then open **System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway**. Setup requires an Apple Silicon Mac running natively, without Rosetta.

## FAQ: What if Setup stops with an error?

Read the named step and press **Show Log**. Missing or old Apple Command Line Tools can be installed or updated through Software Update. A failed build leaves an earlier installation in place.

## FAQ: Why does the update check have no answer?

It could not reach GitHub. Press it to retry when your connection is working. For a failed update, choose **Open log** and include that log with your report.

## FAQ: Why isn't Discord showing my game?

Turn on **Display & performance > Online > Discord status**, and run the Discord app on the same Mac. Switching the setting off clears the status.
