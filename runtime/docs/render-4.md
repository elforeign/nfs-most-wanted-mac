- Rain ripples on wet roads (`materials.m` payload 2; `NFS_RAIN_RIPPLES=x360|pc|port`; F10 Graphics > Rain ripples, restart to change provider): complete console installations keep the X360 default and original constants/frames. Without them, the default stays PC (still reflection). Explicit `port` opts into 16 authored 128x128 RGBA8 frames and authored constants, generated without game inputs by `tools_snapshot/rain_ripples_gen.py`. The Marshall-Palmer impact distribution is volume-flux-normalized to 10 mm/h; a nominal 1 mm water film and 20 mm gravity-capillary carrier yield a 0.870213 s loop (about 18.3863 fps). These reference conditions, momentum coupling, packet envelope, channel encoding and glint budget are authored choices, not measured game weather. R encodes bounded unsigned lookup displacement; G minus B encodes signed glint; alpha is ignored. Two repeated layers use a 0.5 m tile and sqrt(2) scale. The original PC lighting/shadow/wetness balance remains; rain zero returns the original reflection. Provider selection freezes before shader warming: all 16 compatible console frames plus both tables, or all authored frames/constants, never a partial mix. Texture slot 15 is a linear RGBA8 array with box-filtered mips. Only wet reflective road surfaces in rain show the effect. `BOOT_DIAG` logs provider, frame count/size, enable state and per-draw rain/frame; `BOOT_DIAG_PROBE=ripples` marks wet road coverage magenta. Offline proof and suppressed-generation control: `runtime/rain_ripples_test.py`. Appearance comparison: `run_shots/ripples_port_vs_pc_vs_console.jpg`; original console reference: `run_shots/rain_ripples_pc_vs_x360.jpg`.

- **Bloom on bright areas** (`NFS_BLOOM_SURFACES=glow|halo|natural`, F10 Graphics > Bloom on bright areas, default `natural` (`settings_port.c:106`                                                                                              ): `natural` conserves energy: scene + 0.20 x (blur(B) - B) at Bloom 100 %, spreading near-white light without lifting a broad bright surface or adding a ring (`x360_post.m:212–217`). The earlier Glow/Halo comparison follows: the maskless bright pass lets any surface above the knee bloom, and the bloom chain lifts an area bigger than its kernel by ~6.2x its bright-pass excess (5x5 gauss and downsample sum 1, the Bloom kernels ~1.96 each, x bloom scale 1.6), so sun-lit snow caps, smoke/spray and lit road strips clip flat, more so as the adapted luminance drops. `halo` subtracts that own lift (the 5x5-blurred bright pass x the kernels' gain, `x_bloom_gain` in `render_post.m`) in the composite, so such areas keep their texture and only the light spilling past their edges is added. Same held frames (15.1.1, 3440x2160; glow / halo / bloom 0): snow cap 30.2 / 0.25 / 0 % clipped, road spray 19.1 / 0.36 / 0.36 %, whole frame at sim 20 s (adapted 0.22) 0.84 / 0 / 0 %. `BOOT_DIAG_LOOK_AB` variants `glow` and `halo`.

- **Wet road reflection** (`roadfilm.m`; F10 Picture): Retail is the default. Water film (`NFS_ROAD_FILM=1`) multiplies only the existing wet reflection RGB by exact unpolarized air/water Fresnel (index 1.33, normal-incidence reflectance 0.020059). It uses the geometric normal and the existing tangent-space eye vector (t4); diffuse, Phong specular, coverage, wetness, fog and ripple controls retain their equations. Both choices apply live through separate shader variants; Retail retains the original shader source byte for byte. Zero-wetness draws select Retail even with Water film enabled. This is an angular Fresnel experiment, without calibrated wet roughness or an energy-partition model. Diagnostics: held `BOOT_DIAG_LOOK_AB=filmoff,filmon` pairs at the same sim tick and exposure.
- Headlight projection (port extra, `headlights.m` / `headlight_profile.c`, hooks in `mirror.c` StuffScenery and `render.m`; **Off by default**, appearance not judged): `NFS_HEADLIGHT_PROJECTION=off|on` (environment, read once). On adds the player's two low beams to opaque world, road and car surfaces in the main view: a candela grid with the R112 cut-off (`headlight_profile.h`), inverse square, receiver cosine, a 100-120 m fade and two 1024x1024 lamp depth maps re-culled each frame from resident scenery and cars. Light is added in renderer units (100 cd/m2 per unit) before fog and exposure; no volumetric beam. The main view is the render-target entry the prepared eView names (eView+0x60); the device snapshot around the lamp pass rebinds through D3D reference counts. At physical scale the beam is faint against the game's authored lighting: the road 8-12 m ahead gets 3.1-3.5 lx (0.001-0.002 units), a wall facing the car at 5-12 m 0.07-0.12 units. In races 10.2.1 and 15.1.1, day and with `NFS_TEST_SUN_ELEVATION=-20`, it adds under 8 levels on road and walls (`run_shots/headlight_projection_ab.jpg`). BOOT_DIAG: a `headlights:` line every 120 frames (valid, lamps, passes, casters, receivers); held pairs `BOOT_DIAG_LOOK_AB=headlightoff,headlighton` (naming `headlighton` runs the feature for the whole run). Separate held passes drift on the car body even with no light (3,804 px > 8 levels for a no-light pass vs 219 for On, race 10.2.1 sim 12), so judge pairs against that floor. Cost (one solo A/B, 3840x2160, 4x MSAA, race 10.2.1, 60 Hz lockstep, sim 20-95 s): CPU busy p50 4.43 -> 4.99 ms, GPU mean 9.02 -> 8.94 ms (no measured GPU increase), 60 fps both. Test: `runtime/headlights_test.py`.

- **Clear coat** (`clearcoat.c`; F10 Cars > Clear coat): port extra, **On by default** (owner's eye); `NFS_CLEARCOAT=off|on` (unset or empty On). On uses separate car t0–t5 variants, including a paired VS/PS for t0; Off preserves the original source and cache key byte for byte. Shadowed t1–t5 views need X360 car paint. Native t0 retains its PC base and cube-s0/diffuse-s1 sampler order in mirrors, reflections and shadowless views. Only `REGPAINT*`, `METPAINT*` and `PEARL*` receive lacquer. Schlick Fresnel uses IOR 1.5 and F0 = 0.04, following [Burley, *Physically Based Shading at Disney*, sections 5.4–5.5](https://media.disneyanimation.com/uploads/production/publication_asset/48/asset/s2012_pbs_disney_brdf_notes_v3.pdf). A power-256 mirror lobe approximates smooth lacquer (about 4.2° half width), rather than a measured roughness or full microfacet BRDF. Both sun and cube use the smooth normal above the flake and normal map. The complete Off result `B` (diffuse, specular/hotspot, flake and original environment) passes through two interfaces: `T = (1−F(N·V))²`. Its ordinary **0.5× env** balance and normal-map **2× authored Fresnel** stay inside `B`. A separate smooth cube uses alpha/AO visibility without those base reflectance masks. Sun share `d = min(F(N·V), F(V·H)·lobe)·lit` and cube share `F−d` partition the coat reflection. Output is `T·B + min((F−d)·env + d·sun, max(limit−T·B, 0))`, with per-channel `limit = max(1, B, env, sun)`. Only the coat addition can be clamped; the base is never normalized. Unit-radiance inputs remain at most one; inherited HDR paint and HDR illumination survive within that input bound. Normalizing the base to one and replacing its environment previously erased bright highlights. At N·V = N·L = 0.7, N·H = 1, the unit-white ordinary fixture has Off diffuse/spec/hotspot/env = 1/1/5/0.5 (total 7.5): old On was 1, fixed On is 6.920781, with diffuse transmission 0.917126 and added coat 0.042333. These fixture normals are not reconstructed from a screen capture. Metal checks reject the shipped highlight loss, grazing darkening and incorrect diffuse attenuation; full-paint radiance bounds, smooth directional-cube reflections, flake, zero exponents, t0 HDR/gating/link and removed-lobe/attenuation/doubled-env controls also pass. Held `BOOT_DIAG_LOOK_AB=coatoff,coaton` pairs share scene and exposure; variants preload even when Off. Garage close-ups keep the base highlight (BMW M3 GTR, Porsche Carrera GT); solo 4K cost pending.
