# Dormant screen motion raster and resolve

`temporal_render.m` supplies dormant motion through `temporal_render_request`, sharing the state/queue opt-in. With no consumer, one integer request test at the draw boundary enters no temporal work: no target allocation, additional shader output, binding or encoder. When requested, actual primary scene draws accept SP/P0/P1 snapshots and write RGBA16Float at host attachment 4 while preserving guest MRT0–3 and their masks. RG is previous-minus-current unjittered local UV, B is expected previous positive view depth (−1 invalid), and A is reactivity. Rigid car parts and wheels use their queued previous world poses; known rigid scenery uses the preceding camera. Unsupported depth winners invalidate metadata, while blended/non-depth-writing draws max-blend A only. Homogeneous clip coordinates interpolate per sample; raster sample positions correct subpixel edge setup. The full-resolution atlas matches actual 1/2/4× colour/depth samples, including A2C; depth clears invalidate the same rectangle. After ending the scene, `render_temporal_motion` selects a sample matching the supplied full raw resolved depth, marks mixed coverage reactive, and commits that accepted view's poses. Velocities are never averaged. At 1× with the original depth surface, the raster texture is borrowed directly without a second target or resolve pass. At 3840×2160/4× the raster target is 265.4208 MB plus 66.3552 MB for its single-sample resolve, shared across split views. The production-shader fixture compiles 108 retail and 91 custom cases and checks analytic motion, occlusion, transparency, clears, MRT preservation, scene ownership and four failing controls. An offline Metal fixture measured a 1.987000 ms paired median increment over 31 warmed pairs (0.712500 ms baseline, 2.715833 ms with motion, FP16 scene colour/4× depth resolve included): the ≤0.3 ms G1 budget is missed. Real-scene solo timing remains pending. No setting or look default changes.
