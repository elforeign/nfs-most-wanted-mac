## Widescreen (`widescreen.c`)

The resolution slots follow the main display: heights 720, 1080, 1440, 1800 and 2160 at its aspect, then its native size, all capped at 4K; sizes above the display's are left out (on a 4K 16:9 TV: 1280x720 to 3840x2160; start-up prints the list). Slot 1, 1080p, is the default (what the game's first-run detection picks). The options menu prints the size; `NFS_WIDESCREEN=off` restores the retail 4:3 list. Wider than 4:3:
- **3D is Hor+:** the game derives its vertical FOV from the viewport (Vert-); the projection of full-screen views (viewport = back buffer) is corrected just before FUN_006CF400's `D3DXMatrixMultiply`, so frustum culling follows, and so does the view's pixel scale that car LODs are picked with (`eView::GetPixelSize`). Mirror, shadow and environment-map views are untouched (`BOOT_DIAG` logs each view type once). The sun and its glare are billboards through the corrected view, so they keep their shape.
- **Raindrops on the lens** (`rain_drop.efx`, clip-space quads as wide as they are tall, so 4:3 ellipses on the retail game) are narrowed by (4/3)/aspect with their lens image, so they keep the 4:3 shape.
- **2D is pillarboxed:** every FEng quad goes through `eViewPlatInterface::Render` (006D1260) from 640x480 FE space under the FE pass's identity WorldViewProj, which becomes an x-scale of (4/3)/aspect.
- **HUD:** from 3:2 it uses the game's own widescreen layout (the X360's: `FEngHud::SetWideScreenMode`, minimap moved out), so it sits at the screen edges. That layout is made for 16:9; on narrower screens (16:10 MacBooks, 3456x2232) the 2D layer is scaled down while racing so it fits (`run_shots/30`).
- **The game's widescreen FE assets:** `eIsWidescreen` (004FB000, false on PC) is true from 3:2, as on X360: `GLOBAL\WIDESCREEN_GLOBAL.BUN` instead of `THINSCREEN_GLOBAL.BUN` (`WS_Loading.fng`), the `WS_` movie and loading packages, `CURRENT_GEN_WIDESCREEN` to the splash and showcase screens (and the tutorials' own message), and the wide title screen (`WS_MW_LS_Splash.fng` in the boot flow, `run_shots/41`). All `WS_` packages are in the PC data (`BOOT_DIAG` probes them at the main menu). `NFS_WS_ASSETS=0` keeps the 4:3 ones. Menus stay pillarboxed: they are the same packages as on X360. **`XENON_WS`:** where the PC sends `CURRENT_GEN_WIDESCREEN`, the X360 sends `XENON_WS` (title, loading, controller loading, showcase), which in `WS_Loading.fng` fades in the backdrop `WS_XENON_SPLASHBACK`; the PC never sends it and lacks the texture, so the wide loading screen was black (`run_shots/42`). `x360_textures.m` now adds X360-only textures at start-up (read from an uncompressed TPK in the X360 files, untiled, registered in the game's extra-texture list that `GetTextureInfo` searches after the packs; `NFS_X360_EXTRA_TEXTURES=off` or `=<x360 files dir>`; `BOOT_DIAG` writes `x360_texture_<hash>.png`), and the wide loading packages get what the X360's loading screens send: `XENON_WS` (`WS_Loading_Controller.fng` also `CURRENT_GEN_WIDESCREEN`), sent when `FEngine::PushPackage` (005C4DF0) has made the package; it shows until the track starts loading, when `FadeScreen.fng` and its own loading bar cover it (both layouts). On PC they got no message at all: the `LoadingScreen` / `LoadingControllerScreen` ctors that send `CURRENT_GEN_WIDESCREEN` are dead code (no screen-factory entry, no callers). `NFS_LOADING_LAYOUT=pc` sends nothing (black backdrop). `NFS_SPLASH_LAYOUT` does the same for the wide title (its X360 backdrop and layout; default `x360` since 2026-10-20, `=pc` keeps the PC layout). The showcase's two scripts are identical for both messages, so it is left alone. Without the texture neither is sent. `run_shots/52`.
- **Boot flow (`bootflow.c`):** with the widescreen assets on and an HD mode, the boot screens are the X360's HD widescreen list: the EA HD bumper (`WS_LS_EA_hidef.fng`, the X360's `eahd_bumper_english_ntsc.wmv`, 4 s, unskippable like the EA logo), then `WS_LS_EAlogo`, `WS_LS_PSA`, `WS_MW_LS_AttractFMV`, `WS_MW_LS_Splash`; the PC's `MC_Bootup` stays first and `MC_Main` / `MainMenu` follow as before. The PC kept the screen, its movie entry and both packages but never queued it, and has no VP6 of the movie: `movies.c` answers the game's `bFileExists` check for movies the X360 folder has, and without the X360 WMV the bumper is left out. `run_shots/51`. `NFS_BOOT_FLOW=pc` keeps the PC list (with the wide title). The wide title package has no PC prompt object, so its X360 prompt object carries the PC text; the X360's START prompt and its ESRB online-play notice are the default since 2026-10-20 (`NFS_SPLASH_PROMPT=pc` keeps the PC text), `=none` leaves the title as the game sets it. The X360's own wide title layout (the `XENON_WS` message, backdrop `WS_XENON_SPLASHBACK`): `NFS_SPLASH_LAYOUT=x360` (above). `BOOT_DIAG` logs the list (`bootflow:` in events.log). The game asks for `MOVIES\<name>_<language>_<region>.vp6`, PAL for every language but English US; a movie installed only in another region or language (a US install played in German, a European one in English US) stands in, and the log says so (`movies: ... is not installed; playing ...`), as it does for a movie with no file at all.
- **Movies and mouse:** every full-screen movie quad (all are overscanned for TVs: y -16..496, the `WS_` ones x -130..770) is fitted to the movie's aspect in the visible area, so X360 movies fill a 16:9 screen with nothing cut (letterboxed on narrower screens); the mouse maps through the same scale.
- **First run:** with no saved resolution the game starts in the display's native slot (the largest, at most 4K                                                                 , `first_run_settings` in d3d9.m; `BOOT_REG=g_RacingResolution=N` wins, the other sizes stay in Video options), not detection's slot 1; the retail default 0 made the whole first session run at 720p (detection doesn't resize).

`BOOT_DIAG` also logs each 2D quad drawn outside the FE pass once (only the sun's two in a race). 1920x1080 races run at ~71 fps (`run_shots/26`-`28`); the native 3456x2232 at 67-71 fps with 3.8 ms of GPU (still CPU-bound).

## Antialiasing (MSAA)

Video options > Antialiasing (`g_FSAALevel`) offers the sample counts the Metal device supports: 0 = off, 2 = 4x is the first-run default since 2026-10-20 (detection would pick 1; overridden after both detections, see Settings), 1 = 2x, 2 = 4x, 3 = 8x on the measured host (start-up prints `antialiasing levels`). `BOOT_REG=g_FSAALevel=N` sets it for one run. The CreateDevice log line shows the game's level, its menu maximum (`0x901890`, from `CheckDeviceMultiSampleType` minus 1 and the vendor/caps table in FUN_006d2100, which never lowers it for the reported GeForce) and the MultiSample type/quality it asked for. Checked 2026-10-20: level 2 gives 4x (race and front end starts), level 3 gives 8x (1080p race: 146 MB, 3.45 ms GPU, 120 fps cap), menu maximum 3. The back buffer, the auto depth buffer and render targets the game creates with MultiSample render into 2DMultisample textures; a resolve pass updates the single-sample copy when something reads it (the scene StretchRect, Present, the X360 chain's DOF depth): 2-3 per race frame. Memory at 4K: 2x 146 MB, 4x 292 MB (back buffer + depth). GPU cost at 4K, back to back with other sessions' games competing (absolute numbers inflated): off 18.6 ms, 2x 21.8 ms (+3.2), 4x 23.5 ms (+5.0) per frame; an uncontended 4K 4x race ran at the 120 fps cap with ~8 ms of GPU. With `BOOT_DIAG`, `stats.log` has `msaa Nx (MB, resolves)` and draw dumps list `msaa resolve:` lines; `render_msaa_samples()` gives the current level to the overlay. Geometry MSAA alone does not smooth alpha-tested foliage. F10 World > Smooth cut-out edges (`NFS_ALPHA_TO_COVERAGE=off|on`, default Off) enables alpha-to-coverage on unblended alpha-tested draws at 2x/4x/8x. It applies live, with separate pipeline keys. 1x, blended draws and non-monotone comparisons keep the exact D3D9 discard. The output alpha is sharpened over one pixel around the same 8-bit threshold; opaque RGB and the threshold contour stay unchanged. The second tree fringe pass and additive grass shells retain their blending. The original shader texkill instructions still apply.

`runtime/alpha_to_coverage_test.py` exercises the production selector, key bytes, descriptor flag and auxiliary binding, then renders a synthetic shader through the real translator at every supported sample count. It checks fractional edges, opaque contrast and integrated silhouette width against the analytic threshold contour, with key-bit, pipeline-flag and discard negative controls. GPU proof is recorded in `.metal-cache/a2c-test-proof.txt`. Subpixel textures whose mip alpha has already lost the wire cannot be reconstructed by this switch.

The PC world/tree effects set ALPHATESTENABLE/ALPHAREF from `BlendState`; the renderer reads states 15/24/25 into `SmPSAux` and performs the 8-bit comparison in the fragment epilogue. This covers cut-out world geometry and tree cards. PC grass uses the same world path; replacement grass shells have their own authored texkill and blending, which remain intact. Diagnostic `a2c:` pipeline lines report the actual sample count, compare, reference, blend and coverage flag. The captured 2x world/tree draws use GREATER 95; 1x shadow draws keep discard.

Two 70-second, lockstep 15.1.1 runs at 2x/1280x720 captured 80 frames each, with the same AI cruise capped near 50 km/h. `run_shots/a2c_ab.jpg` contains crops and measurements reproduced by `runtime/a2c_pan_metric.py OFF_DIAG ON_DIAG OUTPUT.jpg`: the starting wooden fence's edge-band temporal MAE is 4.313 -> 4.244 (-1.59%), with 99.75% of its P95-P5 contrast retained. Two foliage regions across seven 8-frame moving bursts give 4.855 -> 4.717 (-2.84%, 97.88% contrast) and 7.160 -> 7.012 (-2.07%, 95.11% contrast). These are raw frame differences in a shared Off-derived edge band, including expected motion and rain; they do not isolate aliasing. The original iron/chain-link view and its 4K motion flicker remain unverified. The two-run budget is exhausted; the setting remains Off for visual judgement.

Offline width reference: an opaque projected strip retains its RGB contrast and integrated pixel coverage as it moves between samples. Synthetic 2.5/4.5/8.5-pixel strips exercise this reference at 2x/4x/8x, alongside 1x/Off controls. The GPU matrix has 224 configurations and 79 fractional edge pixels; the maximum single-edge contour error is 0.251 pixels and maximum per-phase strip-width error is 0.5 pixels (at 2x); the maximum error averaged across four subpixel positions is 0.125 pixels. Both opaque RGB channels remain exact. Missing pipeline coverage and old discard controls fail, rather than silently producing a softer/fainter strip. Verification: recompiler build has zero compile errors, runtime links successfully, and the whole offline suite reports 159 passing tests in 281 seconds with four existing environment/input skips (`gpu_sandbox_test`, `test_app`, `test_release_artifact`). The key-bit control has one failure; disabled-coverage and old-discard GPU controls have 94 and 1197 failures. No gate or additional game launch was run.

## Glow behind signs (`decal_depth.m`)

Built in; no toggle. The Bank of Rockport sign (opaque, z-write on, LESSEQUAL) and the LED panel behind it (additive SRCALPHA/ONE, z-write off, LESSEQUAL, drawn after the sign) lie in one plane within depth resolution, so the LED shows through the sign when still and flickers with a few centimetres of movement. Retail flags no background layer (FUN_006c68b0: every non-zero TextureInfo blend type, +0x56, turns z-write off; additive decals that lie on a surface share it), so the fix pushes only the listed panel textures (name hash, tagged on the D3D texture at TextureInfoPlatInterface::Init) back by 16 depth-bias units of 2^-24 plus slope 1. Check: `runtime/signled_check.sh <scratch>` (two guarded runs at the sign; the strip is compared with the measured retail reference); `--counts` recounts without launching.

## Display changes mid-run (`user32.m` display_poll, `widescreen.c`)

The main display can change under a running game (a TV waking, mirroring). `NSApplicationDidChangeScreenParametersNotification` marks it; 0.7 s after the last one (a debounce for the burst), and not while the F10 menu is open, full screen windows re-fit the screen. If the game is on the native resolution (and `g_RacingResolution` isn't a BOOT_REG override, Options not mid-edit), the resolution list is rebuilt for the new display and the game's own resolution change runs (`LoadedFlagMaybe` 982C39: eDisplayFrame resets the device at the new size). A resolution picked in Options stays. Log: `boot: display changed`, `boot: native resolution A -> B`, then the new swap chain. Test: `NFS_TEST_DISPLAY_CHANGE=<secs>:<WxH>` (with `NFS_DISPLAY_PIXELS` for the start size) pretends the display became WxH pixels after that many seconds.

| Internal switch | Default | Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| `NFS_POST_CUTS` | `0` | `1`: discard the final treatment load for complete non-split targets; use 25 weighted bilinear curve taps at 3840×2160 only. Discard ordinary coverage D16 after use; resolve R8 without storing MS samples only for empty coverage, resetting it to Clear for any continuation. Nonempty MS masks retain their samples for incremental reuse; split/partial-source, proof and nonempty reuse paths retain all stores. Off retains the legacy shader source and attachment actions. |

## Traffic detail

F10 Cars > Traffic detail (`NFS_TRAFFIC_DETAIL=off|on`) starts On (a saved Off stays) and applies live. On applies only to AI traffic (PVehicle driver class 2); player Challenge Series GARB/TAXI and front-end cars keep their original glass and LOD. CarRenderInfo reads its vehicle at +0x88 and the driver class at vehicle +0x94, then carries traffic role in render flag 0x80000. On aliases TRAFFICWINDOWS (`1FF8C329`) to the existing WINDSHIELD selection for both physical car materials and X360 car glass. It reuses the windshield's measured optics or generated alpha/reflection values; the Car glass and Physical car materials choices still determine the treatment. Off preserves the original traffic material. No textures or meshes are replaced.

On routes the traffic branch of CarRenderInfo::Render through its existing racer LOD selection, including the first comparison: 120/25/20/10 projected pixels instead of 80/40/20/10. Loaded minimum/maximum LOD clamps, traffic flags and environment-map cutoff remain original. This retains LOD B in the 25–40 pixel band, but selects B earlier in the 80–120 pixel band; it is the racer table rather than a uniform distance multiplier. PC and X360 retail look presets set this optional choice Off; My picks can save On.

`traffic_detail_test.py` compiles the production glass filters and translated x87 selection/clamp instructions. Across 16,657 assertions it checks both modes, glass selection/car gating, every integer pixel size 1–130, racer/traffic paths, loaded bounds and preserved flags/stack. The original paths produce 603 failures; the enabled implementation produces zero. `traffic_role_test.py` also executes the translated role decoder and LOD instructions for absent vehicles and driver classes 0–4, both settings, loaded base LODs 0/2, and all integer pixel sizes 1–130: 21,840 assertions, zero failures; the reviewed pre-fix glass alias fails 2,600. Glass values in this bare fixture are deliberately distinct synthetic values to prove that the existing row is reused, rather than introducing new optical constants.

## Traffic sedan pilot

Windows does not support the pilot setting or registration; retail fallback bodies retain retail traffic even when On is requested.

Private F10 Cars > Traffic sedan pilot (`cars.traffic_pilot`,
`NFS_TRAFFIC_PILOT=off|on`) starts **Off** and takes effect after restart.
The later-car master and Traffic detail do not enable it. PC and Xbox 360 retail
look presets select Off; My picks can retain On. An explicit environment value
has the normal settings override priority. The public build has no row or
activation, including with saved preferences or an environment override.

`traffic_pilot.h` freezes the generic alias TRAFFICSEDAN, type 160, and native
donor TRAF4DSEDA, type 25. `private_cars_boot` pins the startup setting after the
private catalog initializes. Availability requires the catalog's approved,
verified optional sedan, with the later-car master enabled. Off never queries
that optional getter; the policy module opens no source or asset files. Public
startup does not inspect the diagnostic environment, guard or inventory.

`BOOT_DIAG_TRAFFIC_CAR=traf4dseda|trafficsedan` requests a diagnostic subject only
with nonempty `BOOT_DIAG` and the existing guarded scratch userdata approval.
An empty, misspelled, unguarded or unavailable pilot request fails explicitly at
startup (exit 2); it never silently uses a different car. The native baseline may
be requested with the pilot Off. The spawn adapter must also surface
`TRAFFIC_PILOT_DIAGNOSTIC_FAILURE` if a requested subject lacks a decoded key or
registered assets. A successful zero/refusal stays zero, including diagnostics.

The pure choice helper accepts decoded keys, asset availability, existing
selection ordinal, counts and limits. Normal policy widens only an already eligible
sedan opportunity: even/odd ordinals select native/pilot when both are available;
a sole available choice remains eligible. It consumes no RNG. Forced choices
may use any successful traffic opportunity, with the same shared sedan quotas. Native
and pilot counts add together for the fixed cap and percentage cap; zero caps
are unlimited. Percentage allowance is the original minimum-one floor of
`population * percentage / 100`. The adapter supplies NextSpawn's decoded
population, including its existing reserve, and must count both identities
before retail quota refusal. The helper never revives a retail refusal.

Runtime integration is **incomplete**: type registration and asset routing, the
NextSpawn shared count/choice hook (00426230), and available-vehicle pool handling
(00426380) remain unconnected. Enabling this setting alone does not spawn the
pilot. Offline tests exercise the production setting declarations, boot pinning,
public exclusion, guarded requests and bounded exhaustive choice/quota inputs,
including zero keys, unavailable assets and wide count arithmetic. Broken
controls must fail alongside passing production checks.

Paint, glass, stance and LOD appearance await visual acceptance. Reuse current
car-glass optics; the existing measured reference is glass n=1.52, F0 approximately
0.043 ([material reference](https://api.physicallybased.info/materials)). This
policy adds no tint or roughness constants. Sound remains donor-inherited.

**Live picture rows** (`live_picture.c`; `.live_env` in `settings.h`): a change to Contact shadows, Soft smoke, Car reflections or Full screen mode exports its switch and applies without a restart. Reflections queue the game's own device Reset (the flag the Video options' Accept sets: effects reloaded, targets recreated, one ~0.4 s frame) and `v_Reset` calls `live_picture_on_reset()` first, which re-reads the state that used to be read at start-up; the two shading switches are read from the environment per call today (a cache for either defines `render_soft_smoke_reload()` / `x360_post_ssao_reload()`, which the change calls when they exist). HDR output and Fog stay restart rows (their shaders are specialised per mode). Test-only `BOOT_KEYS` step `set:<row id>=<saved spelling>` (for example `set:graphics.look_reflections=pc`) changes a row as the menu does. Check: `runtime/live_picture_check.sh <dir>` (one guarded race; `NFS_TEST_LIVE_PICTURE_BROKEN=1` is the failing control); offline `live_picture_rows_test.py`, `settings_live_test.c`. Full screen mode: Native leaves or enters its Space, no device Reset.

**Live undo sweep:** `runtime/live_picture_check.sh --sweep <new-dir>` sets every live value row (63, hidden ones too)
to a non-default value and back in guarded races, comparing with an untouched baseline (chase tolerance 3%);
18 commands and derived rows (`Setting.derived`, e.g. Look preset) are listed, not set. Failed batches split to name
rows; coupled rows (window mode, full screen) never share a batch. Seed: `--skip-undo graphics.brightness` must FAIL
by name. Rules: `live_picture_sweep.py`.
