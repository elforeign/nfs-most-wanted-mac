- **Car-paint nonfinite fix (`materials.m`, 2026-10-28):** the offending operation is Metal `powr(0,0)` in both specular lobes. Decoded input: `PLAINNOTHING` / hash `010CB64A`, material field +0x58 `SpecularPower=0`; the saturated reflection/light dot is `rv=0`, hence both exponents `sp` and `80*sp` are zero. This produces NaN before the final colour calculation. The X360 09bb919c shader instead executes finite `LOG_CLAMP -> MUL -> EXP`: `log2(0)` clamps to `-FLT_MAX` (the helper's misleadingly named `FLT_MIN` is bits `ff7fffff`, not positive FLT_MIN), multiplication by zero yields signed zero, and `exp2(0)=1`. - **World lighting** (`lighting.c`, `NFS_WORLD_LIGHTING=neutral|original`, default **Neutral**,                                                   F10 Graphics, live): Neutral targets the **direct sun colour**, not grey ambient-plus-sun ground. Its source Planckian CCT follows a piecewise linear elevation curve (degrees/K): 5/3500, 15/4500, 30/5300, 62/5800, constant outside those endpoints. This is an artistic curve using the specified artistic endpoints, not a measured weather model. [Air mass increases as the sun lowers](https://pvpmc.sandia.gov/modeling-guide/1-weather-design-inputs/irradiance-insolation/air-mass/); [Kang et al. 2002's Planckian xy approximation](https://colour.readthedocs.io/en/develop/_modules/colour/temperature/kang2002.html) supplies the chromaticity. With source and D65 normalized to Y=1, `XYZ'=.85*sourceXYZ+.15*D65XYZ` gives 15% partial adaptation (equivalent to partial Bradford adaptation of the source white). Adapted direct CCT is approximately 3796/4736/5459/5899 K at those elevations. Ambient is desaturated two thirds toward its own linear luminance (`Y=.2126R+.7152G+.0722B`, `RGB'=RGB+(2/3)*(Y-RGB)`), preserving shade brightness and ambient W. Across the active PC/X360 quartet and tested blends shade is 6943–8451 K, Duv +.00013 to +.00378, below the 10000 K ceiling and within the non-magenta locus band [0,.006]. This amount also brings the most blue/magenta authored ambient above the Planckian locus; the previous 50% preset did not.

  The direct RGB is normalized to Y=1 and scaled to the game's original direct luminance after `min(1,k*sunRGB)`. If any channel would exceed 1, one shared scalar is reduced so the brightest channel reaches 1. This keeps colour, all direct channels in [0,1], and the closest feasible ground luminance. At k=1 the maximum tested ground shortfall is documented                                                                 ; shade luminance is always preserved. At low sun the lit-ground sum remains mostly skylight and may be cool; it is never bluer than shade in the offline matrix. The flat-ground reference is `ambient*W*.27 + direct*N.L`, with normalized sun-direction Z as N.L; fog, specular, coloured prelight and postprocessing affect the final appearance. In-game only: Original and the front-end garage keep authored lighting. Baked vertex/street/tunnel lamp colours, cars (own light context), sky (CloudIntensity) and Tunnel shadows are unchanged. Ambient balancing is part of Neutral; the separate Shade colour menu/env option has been removed, and stale saved shade preferences have no consumer. `BOOT_DIAG` logs `lighting_options:` ambient before/after once per in-game load and `lighting:` TOD/direct colour every five seconds; `BOOT_DIAG_LOOK_AB` retains `wlorig` / `wlneutral`.
