# Guide

Your Carbon installation uses your own copy of Need for Speed Carbon (PC).

## Open Help from the couch

Click the touchpad on a DualSense or DualShock 4 to open F10. On a keyboard, press F10, or Fn+F10 if F10 controls your Mac's sound.

Choose **Help > Guide**.

On a controller without a touchpad, use its Guide / Home button or hold View.

## Help

- **Guide** opens this document. **Last second** and **Audio output** show recent performance and sound information.
- Under **Report a problem**, choose **What's wrong** and add an optional **Note** describing what happened.
- **Save report** collects a screenshot, recent performance, logs and settings. **Show reports in Finder** opens the saved folder.
- **Show logs in Finder** opens the current logs.

## Graphics and sound additions

F10 **Picture** offers **Look preset: Port / PC retail / My picks**. Port restores
shipped graphics defaults; PC retail turns off the port graphics additions. Changing
a graphics addition remembers My picks, which you can restore after trying a preset.
Presets leave native Video, HDR output, sound and controller choices alone.

- **Car lacquer**, **Physical materials** and **Contact shadows** start On.
  Physical materials require Car lacquer. **Sharper reflections** starts Off.
- **Day-night lighting** starts Off. **Game** follows game progress, **Cycle** advances
  during free roam, and **Fixed** uses **Fixed hour** (initially 12:00).
  Mode and hour take effect with day-night lighting enabled. Imported-world-only
  controls are absent from standalone Carbon because their world data is not installed.
- F10 **Audio** offers **Peak limiter** (On) and **Speaker layout** (Game).
  **ITU** opts into home 5.1 speaker geometry. Choose Stereo or Surround in the
  game's native Audio menu; Surround remains the default for a fresh profile.
- F10 **Controls > Adaptive triggers** starts On for supported controllers.
  Rumble keeps its separate setting.

These additions are saved for the next launch. Use **Restart game** under
**Display & performance**, or quit and reopen the game. **Reset** on a row restores
its shipped default. A switch supplied by a launcher takes priority and locks its row.
Native Video and Audio settings keep their own controls and saved values. The simulation
rate is unchanged.

## Recording and replay

In **Display & performance**, choose **Clip resolution**, then select **Video clip** to start or stop recording. Clips include stereo game sound and are saved in **Movies/NFSC**. **Show clips in Finder** opens that folder. Recording starts Off each launch; the default resolution is 1440p.

**Replay buffer** keeps the last 30 seconds without continuously saving a file. Arm it, wait for warm-up, then choose **Save last 30 s**. Recording, replay and live streaming can share capture. Disarming replay leaves the other actions running.

Under **Controls**, **Replay save key** enables F8, F7 or F6. A saved game binding keeps priority. **Replay save chord** offers L1 + R1 + L3 or L1 + R1 + R3. Both shortcuts start disabled, and only save while driving with the menus closed. Release held buttons after a focus change or reconnect.

## YouTube live

Under **Display & performance > Live streaming**, choose **Enter stream key**. The secure sheet keeps the key only until quit; saving it does not start broadcasting. **Go live** explicitly starts video and stereo sound uploads. **Stop live** stops uploads while recording and replay continue. **Forget stream key** stops streaming and clears the key. Check **Stream status** for progress or errors; an error needs another explicit Go live.

## Controller text entry

Selecting the report's **Note** with a controller opens the on-screen keyboard. It also follows native game text fields. Use the D-pad or left stick to choose a key, Cross / A to type, Circle / B to delete (hold to repeat), and R3 alone to clear on release. The keyboard includes accept, cancel, shift and symbols. Cancel restores a note's original text; native fields keep the game's own validation, accept and cancel actions. Password fields remain masked.

## Licences and performance

**Help > Licences** shows the bundled notices. **Last second** shows recent frame-rate information; **Audio output** shows the current sound format and device. Choose **Performance overlay** under Display & performance for Compact or Full details; it starts Off.
